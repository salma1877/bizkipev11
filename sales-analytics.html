How to read these numbers

  • Invoiced value is the total of non-draft invoices, not cash received.
  • Received so far is the larger of the payments you recorded for an invoice and its income entry, or the full total if the invoice is marked paid. You record these yourself; nothing is checked against M-Pesa or a bank.
  • Outstanding is invoiced value less received so far.
  • Quotation conversion uses each quotation's latest accepted or rejected answer; pending quotations are excluded.
  • The weighted pipeline multiplies accepted, not-yet-invoiced quotations by the planning factor you set. It is your own assumption, not a prediction.

Reports are based on your own Supabase records. No external bank or M-Pesa verification is performed.
