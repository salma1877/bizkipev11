How conversion works

  1. Share a quotation using Online Quotation Approval.
  2. Wait for the customer to accept or reject it.
  3. Convert accepted quotations into draft invoices here.
  4. Open the invoice in your dashboard, set its due date, check the details, then send it using your normal workflow.

Conversion copies the quotation's items, totals, customer details and document data. The due date is left blank for you to set, because a quotation's validity date is not a payment deadline. It does not create a payment, send a message, or confirm that money has been received.
