Report methodology

This report uses the sales and expenses you have recorded in BizKipe. Net recorded result is a simple ledger difference, not a full accounting profit or verified cash-flow statement. Invoice totals (drafts are left out) and payment entries are shown separately because they may overlap with your recorded sales. Different currencies are never added together. Previous-period comparison is available for fixed date windows; “all records” has no equal-length comparison.
