Privacy Notice

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Owner action: This starter notice must be reviewed and adjusted to match the services, analytics, email provider and retention practices you actually use. It is not legal advice.

Information used

The free document generator runs in your browser. Do not enter sensitive personal or financial information into documents unless you need it. The current starter does not save free documents to a BizKipe account.

If you purchase a digital product, the checkout collects your name, email address, M-Pesa phone number, order status and payment reference details needed to process and support the order.

Why information is used

Order details are used to initiate and reconcile payment, deliver the purchased file, prevent fraud, maintain order records and respond to support requests.

Service providers

Configured orders may be processed by Vercel for hosting, Supabase for database and private file storage, and Safaricom Daraja for M-Pesa payment processing. Their own privacy terms also apply.

Security and retention

Server credentials should remain in protected server environment variables. Order records may be retained for accounting, dispute resolution and security purposes. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Your requests

To ask about your order data or request a correction where applicable, contact BizKipe using the details on the Support page. Do not send your M-Pesa PIN or passwords.

Cookies and analytics

This starter does not intentionally add third-party advertising analytics. If you add analytics, cookies, email marketing or other integrations later, update this notice and provide any consent required by law.
