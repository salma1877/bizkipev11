Record completeness checklist

  • Record all business income, including cash and bank receipts not yet entered.
  • Record business expenses and retain receipts, invoices and proof of payment.
  • Check customer invoice payments against your actual bank or M-Pesa statements.
  • Keep personal and business transactions distinguishable.
  • Ask a qualified Kenyan tax professional to review applicable tax treatment and filing obligations.
Tax preparation note: This report is an organisational summary of records entered into BizKipe. It does not calculate KRA tax, VAT, turnover tax, allowable deductions or filing amounts. It is not an official tax return, audited statement or substitute for professional tax advice.

Amounts are not converted between currencies. Invoice payments are counted on the recorded payment date. Unpaid balances are displayed separately. Manually entered records may be incomplete or inaccurate.
