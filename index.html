Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know to get paid faster.

Is BizKipe free?

The invoice and quotation generator is free. Premium templates are KSh 150 and KSh 250 with instant M-Pesa checkout and download.

How do I save my document?

Choose “Print / Save PDF”, then select “Save as PDF” in your browser’s print dialog. On mobile, this option may be under the share or print menu.

Does it send invoices to customers?

This version creates a professional printable PDF in your browser. You can then send it via WhatsApp or email to your customer. Save as PDF and share instantly.

Can I accept M-Pesa?

Yes! BizKipe accepts M-Pesa. Click Buy pack, enter your phone number, and confirm the M-Pesa prompt on your phone. Download is instant after payment.
