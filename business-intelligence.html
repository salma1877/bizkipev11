Data coverage & limitations

  • Sales, expenses and margin use manually entered financial records. Missing records make these totals incomplete.
  • Gross margin estimate = recorded sales less recorded cost amounts; it is not net profit and excludes overheads, taxes and other costs unless entered.
  • Top customers are grouped by the name you entered, ignoring capital letters and extra spaces; other spelling differences stay separate. Expense groups come from simple word matching on your descriptions.
  • Inventory rankings count recorded units deducted through confirmed inventory sales.
  • When all currencies is selected, this page shows counts only and does not add unlike currencies.
  • No M-Pesa, bank, tax, or external provider verification is performed.
