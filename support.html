  • Your order ID, if you placed an order.

  • A short description of the issue and when it happened.

Common questions

Can I use the free document tool without paying?

Yes. The invoice and quotation builder is free to use. Only the premium template pack is paid.

Where do I enter my M-Pesa details on an invoice?

In the invoice builder, fill in the "How to pay" box with your Paybill, Till, send-money number or bank details. They print on your invoice with the amount and due date.

My M-Pesa prompt did not appear

Confirm that the phone number is correct and can receive M-Pesa prompts. Do not submit repeated payments if you are unsure whether the first request succeeded; check the order status first.

I paid but cannot download

Sometimes a payment takes a few minutes to be confirmed. Keep your order ID, and if the download still does not work, contact support with it.
