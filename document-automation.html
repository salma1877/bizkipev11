How document numbering works

Each schedule uses its own prefix and sequence. For example, BK-REC-2026-0001. The sequence advances only after the draft invoice is successfully created. Numbers are not guaranteed to be unique across different schedules, so choose a distinct prefix for each one.

Generating a recurring invoice copies customer, line items, prices, tax and branding from the selected saved invoice. Review the draft for price changes, service delivery and tax accuracy before sending it.

BizKipe · Recurring invoice generation is manual. No invoice is automatically sent and no payment is automatically collected or verified.
