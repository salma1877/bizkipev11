Method and limitations
  • Historical averages use the sales and expenses you have recorded, grouped by calendar month and currency. The current month is included even though it is not finished, so it can pull the averages down.
  • The selected scenario multiplies historical average sales by 80%, 100% or 120%. These are user-selected planning assumptions, not AI predictions.
  • Expenses use the monthly budget you enter; they are not forecast from historical expenses.
  • Estimated ending cash is a simple opening cash estimate plus projected net movement. It excludes receivables, payables, tax, loans, owner withdrawals and unrecorded transactions.
  • Different currencies are never added together. No payment settlement or M-Pesa balance is verified.
