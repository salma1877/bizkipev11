Transparency: this release uses a built-in, rule-based assistant. It does not send your business data to an AI provider and does not use an external AI API. It can only answer supported questions from records it successfully loads. It is not an accountant or financial adviser.

Data limits: figures may be incomplete if records are missing. Margin is sales less entered cost amounts, not net profit. Amounts still to receive are the sale amount less any amount you recorded as received. Customer names are grouped ignoring capital letters and extra spaces. Currency totals are never combined across different currencies. No M-Pesa or bank payment verification is performed.
