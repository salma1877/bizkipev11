About BizKipe

BizKipe is a free online tool for making professional invoices and quotations. It is built for freelancers and small businesses in Kenya, and it works well on a phone.

Why I built it

Many small businesses still write invoices by hand, in a chat message, or in a document that looks different every time. That makes it harder to look professional, and harder to get paid. I wanted a simple tool that anyone could open on their phone, fill in, and send in a few minutes, without paying or learning accounting software.

What you can do with it

  • Create invoices and quotations with automatic totals, and save or print them as PDF.
  • Add a "How to pay" box with your M-Pesa Paybill or Till number, send-money number or bank details, so clients know exactly where to send the money.
  • Save your documents and keep track of clients, expenses and reports in a dashboard.
  • Read the free guides on invoicing, quoting and getting paid on time in Kenya.

Who is behind it

BizKipe is built and run by Salma Chieng in Kisumu, Kenya. I am a developer and educator, and I build BizKipe myself, mostly from my phone. I also run TermuxX, a free WhatsApp channel that teaches beginners about tech.

Find me on LinkedIn or join the TermuxX WhatsApp channel.

BizKipe is a young product and it improves based on what real users ask for. The "How to pay" box, for example, was added after a user pointed out that an invoice should tell clients how to pay.

How BizKipe handles your information

Your documents belong to you. We explain what we collect and why in our Privacy policy, and the rules for using the service are in the Terms. BizKipe helps you prepare business documents. It does not give legal, tax or accounting advice, so check your own obligations.
