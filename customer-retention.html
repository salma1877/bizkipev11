How segments are calculated

  • Loyalty reward ready: available points meet the reward threshold from your existing loyalty settings and records.
  • Highest recorded spend: customers in the top 20% of recorded invoice payments, calculated separately in the configured loyalty currency.
  • At risk: customers with at least one invoice and no invoice dated in the last 90 days.
  • Repeat: customers with two or more saved invoices.
  • New: customers with exactly one saved invoice.
These are simple operational rules. They do not establish customer consent, guarantee future purchases or send messages. Review contact details and applicable consent requirements before contacting anyone.
